What Star Naming Really Means

We believe a meaningful gift starts with transparency, so here it is, plainly.

  • Symbolic naming. Star names in our registry are symbolic and treasured by gift recipients worldwide. They are not recognised by the IAU or the scientific community, and we'll never pretend otherwise.
  • A real, permanent record. Your chosen name, the star's catalogue ID and its coordinates are stored permanently in the Astralis registry.
  • What you receive. A personalised certificate, the star's precise celestial coordinates, a custom star map, and luxury packaging on Premium and Ultimate tiers.
  • Risk-free. 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. If the moment isn't perfect, we'll make it right.

Frequently Asked

You explore our interactive galaxy, choose an unnamed star, and give it the name of your choice. We record the name, the star's catalogue ID and its coordinates permanently in the Astralis registry, then produce your personalised certificate. The naming is symbolic, a beautiful gesture rather than an official astronomical designation, and we're upfront about that on every certificate.

Digital certificates are delivered to your inbox within minutes of ordering, perfect for last-minute gifts. Printed packages ship within 1 to 2 business days, and Ultimate orders receive priority processing. Tracked shipping across Australia is included.

The Astralis registry is our permanent database of every named star. Each entry records the star's unique ID, its right ascension and declination, the name you chose and the date of dedication. Recipients can look up their star at any time using the ID on their certificate.

The Standard package is fully digital: a high-resolution PDF certificate and star map delivered instantly. Premium adds a museum-quality printed certificate in a luxury presentation folder. Ultimate includes a framed certificate inside a keepsake gift box, ready to hand over.

Beautifully. Printed certificates arrive on heavyweight archival paper with gold foil detailing, wrapped in tissue inside a navy presentation folder. The Ultimate box opens to reveal the framed certificate beneath a vellum star map. No prices appear anywhere in the package.
